Introduction

What is Sakeenah?

Sakeenah (سكينة) is the Arabic word for the tranquillity that settles on the heart. The app is built around that idea: the essentials of a Muslim's day in one calm place, without noise, feeds or sign-ups.

Open it and you see the next prayer and how long until it begins. From there it is one tap to the Quran, with Uthmani script, an English translation and recitation by seven renowned reciters; to the morning and evening athkar from Hisn al-Muslim; to a tasbih that keeps count; or to a simple log of the prayers and fasts you have kept.

Prayer times, the Quran text, the duas and your records all live on your phone, so everything except audio recitation works offline. There is no account to create, and it is free.

It is made for everyday use by anyone: someone who has prayed all their life, or someone taking their first steps, for whom there is a gentle guide for new Muslims.

Features

Everything for the day, nothing in the way

Each part of the app does one job well, and all of it is a tap or two from the home screen.

Prayer times

  • Accurate times for your location, calculated on your phone
  • 11 calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr and high-latitude rules
  • An alert as each prayer begins, switchable per prayer
  • Hijri date with manual adjustment
  • Rather not share your location? Enter coordinates by hand

The Holy Quran

  • Full Uthmani script in the Amiri Quran typeface
  • Saheeh International English translation
  • Verse-by-verse recitation by 7 reciters, playing on in the background with lock-screen controls
  • Mushaf page view, search, bookmarks and “continue reading”

Athkar and duas

  • Hisn al-Muslim: all 131 chapters with Arabic, transliteration and translation
  • Morning and evening athkar on tap-to-count cards, with optional reminders
  • Duas from the Quran, and favourites for the ones you return to
  • Ruqyah: Quranic verses and prophetic duas for healing

Tasbih

  • A digital counter with goals and gentle vibration
  • Session history and running totals
  • Build your own dhikr sets and run them as a guided counter

Worship tracker

  • Log each prayer as on time, late or missed, and keep your streak
  • Fasting log, with a guide to the sunnah fasts
  • Qada counter for prayers you are making up
  • Khatmah planner: set a daily goal and complete the Quran

Qibla

A compass that shows the bearing and distance to the Kaaba from wherever you are, and works without a connection.

Ramadan and zakat

  • Ramadan hub with a countdown, suhoor and iftar times and daily tools
  • Zakat calculator with a nisab check and saved history

Learning and reflection

  • The 99 Names of Allah with their meanings
  • A verse and a hadith for each day
  • Step-by-step Hajj and Umrah guide
  • A gentle guide for new Muslims

On your home screen

Widgets for the next prayer and the verse of the day, so a glance is enough. Light, dark and pure-black (AMOLED) themes to suit your eyes and your screen.

Made to respect you

Your worship stays on your phone

In short, and in plain words. The full privacy policy says the same in detail.

Private by design

Your prayer log, bookmarks, tasbih counts and settings are stored on your phone. There are no accounts and no analytics, and none of your worship records are sent to us.

Works offline

Prayer times are calculated on the device, and the Quran, its translation and the duas are built in. Only audio recitation needs a connection; it is streamed from the Islamic Network CDN.

One small, honest ad

A single banner on the main tabs keeps Sakeenah free. It never appears while you read the Quran, make dhikr or pray, and ads are limited to family-suitable content. Where the law requires it, you choose whether ads are personalised, and can change that choice in About.
